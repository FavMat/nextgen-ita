About
Solar 5195
An interactive 3D simulation of our Solar System, built for anyone curious about space. Every planet is positioned using NASA JPL Keplerian elements, accurate from 1800 to 2050. The Moon follows the ELP2000 analytical theory by Jean Meeus. Set the date to any day in history and watch the cosmos align.
Solar System
Eight Planets, One Star
Our Sun holds 99.86% of the Solar System’s mass. The inner rocky worlds — Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars — are dwarfed by the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn, and the ice giants Uranus and Neptune. Beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, home to Pluto and countless frozen remnants of the early Solar System.
Click any planet to see its real data. Advance time to watch orbital mechanics in action — Mercury completes a full orbit in 88 days, while Neptune takes 165 years.
