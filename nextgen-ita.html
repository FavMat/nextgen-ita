Where we begin. And why now.

Matteo Favilli Salvatore Rosania 1 min May 2026

The premise is simple. Italy has talent, has builders, has people doing serious work with AI. What it does not have is a single place where those conversations happen, unedited, in public. This podcast is the first attempt at that place.

Each episode is one conversation with one founder or builder. No script. What we ship, what we kill, what we learned the hard way.

iii.

Free coaching
for founders.

One session, sixty minutes, no upsell. Strategy, AI adoption, or the question keeping you up at 3 AM. 4 slots a month.

→ Act i · Application

You write one email.

Three questions, three short answers. What you are building, the real blocker, why this conversation matters this week. No forms, no calendars, no funnel.

→ Act ii · Review

I read it within 48 hours.

If there is signal, you hear back with a few open slots. If we are not a fit, I point you somewhere better. Either way, you get a reply.

→ Act iii · The call

Sixty minutes on Google Meet.

Italian or English. You bring the context, I bring the questions. We work the actual problem. No chitchat, no fifteen-minute recap at the end.

→ Act iv · The note

You leave with three things to do.

A short follow-up with the calls we made, what to test first, what to ignore. Written so you can act on it Monday morning, not next quarter.

→ Act v · What's next

You ship. That's it.

One session, on purpose. If you want to come back later with new context, the inbox is open. The point is not the meeting, the point is what you do after.

NO CALENDARS · NO FORMS · JUST HUMANS

Not for everyone.

→ YES, apply if
  • You've started building. Even a hacky MVP counts.
  • You have a specific question, not "let's chat".
  • You're ready to be honest about what is not working.
  • You will actually do something with what we discuss.
  • You're Italian, Italy-based, or building for Italy.
→ NOT a fit if
  • You want someone to validate your idea without pushback.
  • You're looking for funding, introductions or a team.
  • You haven't started building. Come back when you have.
  • You want to "pick my brain" about AI in general.
  • You expect a second, third, fourth session.
